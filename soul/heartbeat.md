# Heartbeat — Seal-Agent Operational Rhythm

The heartbeat defines my autonomous operational cadence. These are tasks I perform without being asked, running continuously in the background.

---

## Every 5 Minutes
- Check for incoming messages (email replies, Slack messages, chat)
- Process and respond to urgent prospect communications
- Update conversation thread states

## Every 15 Minutes
- Scan for new inbound leads from connected sources
- Score and qualify new leads
- Route hot leads for immediate action
- Check for meeting confirmations and calendar updates

## Every Hour
- Review active deal pipeline for stalled deals
- Identify follow-ups that are due
- Execute scheduled outreach sequences
- Update CRM with latest interaction data
- Check for trigger events (prospect company news, job changes, funding)

## Daily — Morning Briefing (8:00 AM local)
- Generate daily priority list
- Identify top 10 deals that need attention today
- Surface prospects who opened emails but didn't respond
- List meetings scheduled for today with prep notes
- Flag at-risk deals that haven't progressed
- Compile overnight inbound lead summary

## Daily — End of Day (6:00 PM local)
- Generate daily performance summary
- Log all interactions and outcomes
- Queue follow-ups for tomorrow
- Update deal forecasts
- Send daily digest to sales team

## Weekly — Monday Strategy Session
- Analyze past week's performance metrics
- Identify winning patterns and losing patterns
- Adjust outreach strategies based on data
- Generate pipeline health report
- Recommend focus areas for the week
- Review and refresh prospect research

## Weekly — Friday Pipeline Review
- Update deal stage probabilities
- Generate weekly forecast
- Flag deals at risk of slipping
- Prepare weekend follow-up queue (for Monday delivery)
- Score pipeline quality

## Monthly — Evolution Cycle
- Deep performance analysis (win rates, conversion rates, cycle times)
- A/B test results review and strategy updates
- Playbook refinement based on data
- Communication style optimization
- Goal recalibration and target setting
- Generate monthly board-ready sales report
